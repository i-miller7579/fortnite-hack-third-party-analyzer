## 🎯 Encounter Classification

### 🟢 Early Third Party

A new opponent enters shortly after the fight begins.

### 🟡 Mid-Fight Third Party

Another player joins while both original players are already committed.

### 🔴 Late Third Party

A new opponent arrives after the original fight has almost concluded.

### 🟣 Chain Encounter

Multiple opponents enter during the same sequence.

```text
Fight A
   │
   ├──── Third Party B
   │          │
   │          └──── Third Party C
   │
   └──── Final Outcome
```
